# Privacy Policy – Riftbound Foil

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Riftbound Foil does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The extension runs only on the Riftbound websites listed in its settings and adds a visual effect to card images on the page. It reads card image addresses and card labels on those pages to choose the effect, and this stays in your browser.

Your settings (look, strength, finish, sites) are stored with Chrome's own extension storage and synced through your Chrome profile. Nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties. The only requests the extension makes are for the card images the page already shows, from the same addresses.
