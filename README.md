# Privacy Policy – Riftbound Foil

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Riftbound Foil is a browser extension that adds tilt, glare and holographic foil effects to Riftbound card images on Riftbound websites.

## Data collection
The extension does not collect, transmit or sell any personal or non-personal data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no user accounts.

## What the extension does on your device
- It runs only on the Riftbound websites listed in its popup, and not in the riftatlas online play client.
- It reads the page locally in your browser (card image addresses and card labels such as name, rarity and foil status) to choose and place the effect.
- It saves only your settings (look, strength, finish, overnumbered cards and which sites are on) in Chrome's extension storage, which Chrome can sync through your profile. The popup also remembers which of its sections you left open, in this browser only.
- It does not send page data anywhere. The only network requests it makes are for the card images the page already shows, from the same addresses. All of its code is included in the extension package.

## Third parties
The extension shares no data with anyone. Links in the popup open the listed Riftbound websites, which have their own privacy policies.
